Privacy policy

ExpenseTrack · last updated 22 October 2026

ExpenseTrack is a personal expense tracker that runs entirely in your browser. There is no ExpenseTrack server: the app talks directly to Google on your behalf, and nothing you see in it is sent to the app's author or to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Gmail, read-only. The app searches your mailbox for bank and card notification emails and statements, and reads them to list your transactions. It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Google Drive, app folder only. Your settings, categories and transactions are saved in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can see. It cannot open any other file in your Drive.

Where your data lives

Only in your Google account (the hidden app folder) and in your browser's local storage as a cache. The app's author has no copy and no access. Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API data

ExpenseTrack's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing the app there also lets you delete its hidden Drive data.

Contact

Questions can be raised on the app's GitHub repository.
